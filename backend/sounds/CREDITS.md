# Sound credits

Clips in this folder come from Wikimedia Commons. They were trimmed, band-limited,
loudness-normalised and resampled to 8 kHz mono for the camera speakers (that is the only
change). `jingle_bells`, `fireworks`, `halloween` and `ghost` are synthesized in
`backend/audiogen.py` (Jingle Bells is a public-domain tune).
